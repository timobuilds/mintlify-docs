This path reuses the field guide rather than duplicating it into a second curriculum. Complete one deliverable per session. The facilitator may adjust timing to the team’s experience.

| Session | Read | Produce | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Scope | [Role](/book/chapter-01), [operating cycle](/getting-started/operating-cycle) | Responsibility map | Who decides what good means? |
| 2. Value | [Economics](/book/chapter-03) | [Opportunity brief](/practice/template-01) | Is the baseline observed or assumed? |
| 3. Behavior | [AI PRD](/book/chapter-04) | [Behavior specification](/practice/template-02) | Could a reviewer test every requirement? |
| 4. Quality | [Trace review](/book/chapter-10), [judges](/book/chapter-11) | [Evaluation workbook](/practice/template-04) | Which failures are still missed? |
| 5. Operations | [Observability](/book/chapter-12) | [Release gate](/practice/template-05) | Who can stop or roll back the release? |

## Foundation completion

Present a go/no-go decision for a narrowly scoped feature. Attach the baseline, requirements, labeled examples, validation results, uncertainty and monitoring owner. A thoughtful no-go decision is a valid outcome.

Do not substitute completion of this exercise for production qualification. The sample cases are too small to establish reliability.
