New readers should follow the [foundation learning path](/course/index). Teams shipping a feature can start with the [PRD](/book/chapter-04) and [evaluation workbook](/practice/template-04). Researchers should inspect the [source records](/library/index).

**Source-reported** means the retrieved source supports the specific statement. **Instructional synthesis** means a handbook recommendation. **Illustrative** means a constructed example, not a measured result.

The four core references were checked on November 1, 2026. Three expose only public excerpts; their paid sections are not represented as verified. Earlier uploaded captures were not re-transcribed for this release. Deferred references remain labeled in the source catalog.
