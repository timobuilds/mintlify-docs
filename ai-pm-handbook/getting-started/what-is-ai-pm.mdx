---
title: What is AI product management?
description: A product discipline focused on outcomes and evidence.
---

AI PM work connects a customer problem to a system whose behavior must be measured in context. The PM needs enough technical understanding to discuss failures with engineering, while keeping user value and decision rights explicit.

Read [the role chapter](/book/chapter-01) for Gupta’s four-role framing and the handbook’s responsibility exercise. Use the [operating cycle](/getting-started/operating-cycle) to connect each decision to an artifact.
