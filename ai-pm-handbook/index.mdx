This public handbook helps product managers turn an AI capability into a testable product decision. Start with a user outcome, specify behavior, inspect failures and decide what evidence justifies a release.

## Foundation release

- [Understand the PM’s role](/book/chapter-01).
- [Define the economics](/book/chapter-03).
- [Write an AI PRD](/book/chapter-04).
- [Review traces and evaluate failures](/book/chapter-10).
- [Check the evaluator](/book/chapter-11).
- [Operate and improve the product](/book/chapter-12).

Follow the [learning path](/course/index), use the [worksheets](/practice/templates), or check the [source library](/library/index).

The running example is a fictional property-shortlist assistant, “Scout.” All sample numbers and traces are constructed for teaching. They are not claims about a commercial product.

Advanced harness engineering, multi-agent architecture, model training and the broader 12-module curriculum are deferred. This release is a handbook, not an enrollment or certification system.
