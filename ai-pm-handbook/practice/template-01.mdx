## Intended use

Define the target customer, job and baseline.

## Worksheet fields

- **Problem statement** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **target segment** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **five supporting observations** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **baseline time/cost/error rate** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **potential impact** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **counterevidence** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **next experiment** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.

## Example

For the Scout: a real-estate agent spends two hours verifying property claims; evidence must establish actual frequency and opportunity cost.

## Quality checklist

- Can another PM reproduce the assessment?
- Are author assumptions labeled separately from observations?
- Are failure cases and human decision rights explicit?
- Does the artifact include one next action and a named owner?
