## Intended use

Decide whether the product can ship.

## Worksheet fields

- **Task success** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **cost per completion** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **latency** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **privacy checks** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **safety failures** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **authorized actions** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **rollback owner** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **monitoring frequency** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **go/no-go** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.

## Example

Use baselines and explicit thresholds; consider test coverage, correlated failures, sampling uncertainty and catastrophic failure severity.

## Quality checklist

- Can another PM reproduce the assessment?
- Are author assumptions labeled separately from observations?
- Are failure cases and human decision rights explicit?
- Does the artifact include one next action and a named owner?
