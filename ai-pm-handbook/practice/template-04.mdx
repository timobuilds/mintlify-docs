## Intended use

Turn observations into measurable regressions.

## Worksheet fields

- **Trace ID** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **user intent** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **tool calls** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **actual outcome** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **reviewer note/open code** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **axial category** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **severity** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **target check** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **human label** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **judge prediction** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **disagreement** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.

## Example

Review roughly 100 traces as a discovery exercise; do not assume this number is statistically sufficient for rare risk estimation.

## Quality checklist

- Can another PM reproduce the assessment?
- Are author assumptions labeled separately from observations?
- Are failure cases and human decision rights explicit?
- Does the artifact include one next action and a named owner?
