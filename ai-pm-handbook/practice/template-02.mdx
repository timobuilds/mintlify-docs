## Intended use

Specify measurable AI product behavior.

## Worksheet fields

- **JTBD** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **available context** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **expected input/output** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **must/must-not behaviors** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **known failures** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **human approvals** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **latency/cost thresholds** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **rollout hypothesis** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.
- **eval criteria** — supply evidence, assumptions, owner and review date.

## Example

Scout must never assert confirmed zoning without a cited source and retrieval date.

## Quality checklist

- Can another PM reproduce the assessment?
- Are author assumptions labeled separately from observations?
- Are failure cases and human decision rights explicit?
- Does the artifact include one next action and a named owner?
