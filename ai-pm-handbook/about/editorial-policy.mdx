title: Editorial and attribution policy
description: How evidence and limits are represented.
---

This handbook contains original educational synthesis and short attributed summaries. It is not an official standard or a substitute for the original sources.

1. Retrieve a source before summarizing it. Record access scope and review date in `data/sources.yaml`.
2. Distinguish source statements, handbook recommendations and constructed examples.
3. Do not infer paid content from an article title or table of contents. Mark inaccessible references explicitly.
4. Do not publish full source captures, transcripts, private user traces or licensed illustrations without permission.
5. Review source changes and user feedback through GitHub pull requests. Revisit changing tooling when the handbook’s instructions depend on it.

The prior archive is the provenance of this release’s architecture and draft prose. Its old ingestion labels do not establish fresh verification. The four core references were rechecked on November 1, 2026; other source records are deferred.
